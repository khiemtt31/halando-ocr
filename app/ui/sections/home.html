Recommended Flow

  1. Choose a PDF or image.
    Upload creates a document and starts an OCR job for you.
  2. Track processing.
    Processing may take a moment while the local OCR worker is running.
  3. Read or search the text.
    Search works after the document has processed text.
  4. Download or clean up.
    Keep the original, download the OCR version when available, or delete the document.

What can I do now?
